\section{Beta functions}
\subsection{Beta functions defined in Arason}

The beta functions at NLO for EW theory before symmetry breaking are defined as:

\begin{align*}
  \beta_{g_1} &= \frac{41}{10} g_1(t)^3 \\
  \beta_{g_2} &= -\frac{19}{6} g_2(t)^3 \\
  \beta_{y_t} &= y_t(t) \left( \frac{9}{2} y_t(t)^2 - \left( \frac{17}{20} g_1(t)^2 + \frac{9}{4} g_2(t)^2 \right) \right) \\
  \beta_{\lambda} &= 12 \lambda(t)^2 - \lambda(t) \left( \frac{9}{5} g_1(t)^2 + 9 g_2(t)^2 \right) + \frac{9}{4} \left( \frac{3}{25} g_1(t)^4 + \frac{2}{5} g_1(t)^2 g_2(t)^2 + g_2(t)^4 \right) \\
  &+ 12 \lambda(t) y_t(t)^2 - 12 y_t(t)^4
\end{align*}

with $SU(2) \times U(1)_Y$ and $t=\ln(\mu)$.


The running of the fundamental parameters is given by

\begin{align*}
  \frac{d g_1(t)}{dt} &= \frac{1}{16\pi^2} \beta_{g_1} \\
  \frac{d g_2(t)}{dt} &= \frac{1}{16\pi^2} \beta_{g_2} \\
  \frac{d y_t(t)}{dt} &= \frac{1}{16\pi^2} \beta_{y_t} \\
  \frac{d \lambda(t)}{dt} &= \frac{1}{16\pi^2} \beta_{\lambda}
\end{align*}
